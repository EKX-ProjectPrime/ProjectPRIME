Guidelines

Leave policies and allocation rules for the management team.

Vacation Leave
  • The same process will be followed as with the agent to open the 2026 VL Calendar for the management team.
  • Short notice leave filed in less than 2 weeks prior to the actual leave will not be allowed.
  • First Come, First Serve.
Allocations
  • APA supervisor & CSR 3 — 1 per day. Avoidance of VL when the other is on RD. Manager Exception approval needed for the latter.
  • CSR 3 — 2 per day.
  • Senior supervisor — 1 per day.
  • If the supervisor is applying for a VL but no one will cover or man the floor, please consider a schedule swap, but still need to secure manager’s approval.
  • New!We will limit the allocation every Tuesday as this falls the same as our staff meeting or MBR.
